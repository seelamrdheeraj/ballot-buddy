The problem

The ballot is the problem. Not you.

23%
of voters 18–29 turned out in the 2022 midterms
The lowest of any age group.

Most say why: confusing ballot language, missed deadlines, and sources they can’t trust. Ballot Buddy fixes all three, so you vote with confidence.

What it does

Enter your ZIP. Everything else follows.

Your ballot

Any ZIP in the U.S. See the candidates and measures you’ll actually vote on.

Plain-English measures

Every measure in 3 bullets, plus what a YES and a NO vote mean.

Deadline reminders

Registration, mail-ballot, and Election Day dates. One tap to get a nudge.

BallotBotPremium

Ask anything about voting. It explains. It never tells you how to vote.

Also: Match, a swipe-to-compare on real candidate positions with names hidden until the end. Follow your representatives and see their votes next to their promises. Two-minute civic lessons, English and Spanish, and listen-instead-of-read.

Our mission

We summarize. You decide.

Clear, unbiased information on candidates, propositions, and laws. No endorsements. Every summary comes from the ballot’s own text.

Questions

Quick answers.

Is it really nonpartisan?

Yes. No endorsements, no partisan ads. Summaries come from the measure’s own text and candidates’ own words.

What do you do with my ZIP?

Find your ballot. That’s it. We don’t sell data.

Is it free?

Yes, with a few ads. Premium ($7.99/month) removes them and adds BallotBot and candidate comparison.
